What to import
  • The game folder is the one with CODE/ECSTATIC.FAN in it. Pick that folder, or a .zip of it; a folder or zip that holds it one level down works too.
  • Ecstatica 1: the DOS release root, with its W folder inside for the 640×480 graphics.
  • Installers, DIRECTX, ISHIELD, .EXE and .DLL files are skipped.
  • Saved games stay in the browser with the game. Removing a game removes its saves.
  • Music needs a General MIDI soundfont in .sf2 format, for example GeneralUser GS. Without one, sound effects and speech still play.
  • Phones and tablets: use Import .zip. Most mobile browsers cannot pick a folder.
